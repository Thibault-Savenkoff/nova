An open image format for photos, screenshots, animations and camera RAW files. This page opens and creates .nova files on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Examples

    Make a .nova

    Your image is encoded in this browser and the .nova file is saved to your downloads. EXIF, XMP and the colour profile are kept for PNG, JPEG, HEIC and CR3 files.

    Adaptive keeps graphics and screenshots exact and compresses photos at the chosen quality; 90 looks identical to the source.

    What only the nova program can do
    • HEIC images can be converted in Safari only, and their HDR rendition is not kept.
    • Other formats opened by the browser (WebP, GIF…) are converted without their metadata.
    • Animations and Live Photos can only be created with the program.
    • RAW files are developed with the camera's look only; the darktable look needs the program.
    • Export is limited to PNG, JPEG and WebP; the program also writes TIFF, AVIF, HEIC and DNG.
    • Encoding and RAW development are slower than in the program: about 20 seconds each for a 24-megapixel RAW file on a recent computer, longer on a phone.
